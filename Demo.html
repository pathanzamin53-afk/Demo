Principal's Desk

Mohd. Anis (Principal) M.A., B.Ed., L.L.B

"Children are in fact the future builders of the nation. Today every caring patriot is worried about the generation that is wandering from the right path. The root cause of it is the lack of man-making education. Indeed, true education includes adoption of all the energy sources existing in man by accumulating them from everywhere and to forward them to their goal. From the very beginning, Bushra Convent School has been attempting to provide a high quality education and creating a sense of character building for their all round development of the student. The school therefore emphasizes the physical, mental, emotional and character development in order to expose children's inherent talents and make them citizens of free India. Very happy news is that the school has given 100% result in the past decade. I wish for the bright future of the students. I hope in future too, the school will achieve an esteemed position and continue to make all efforts to retain the education standard and inherent quality with full determination. With best wishes"

Admission Procedure

📋 How to Apply

👆

Step 1 — Application FormObtain the application form from the School office or website bushraconventintercollege.in

📁

Step 2 — Document SubmissionTC, Birth Certificate, Aadhaar Card (ward & parents), P.E.N. No., passport-size photographs.

💳

Step 3 — Fee PaymentPay admission fee at the fee counter in School.

✅

Step 4 — Enrollment ConfirmationReceive enrollment confirmation and study materials.

📤 Withdrawal / TC Policy

✍️

TC application must be made in writing on the prescribed form from the School office.

📅

One month's written notice required before mid-session withdrawal. All dues for that month will be charged.

🚫

No TC granted unless all dues are cleared and "No Dues Certificate" obtained from fee counter, Librarian, Lab Incharge and Game Master.
